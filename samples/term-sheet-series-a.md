# Series A Preferred Stock — Summary of Terms

substitute for the NVCA model documents or counsel.

**Company:** Northwind Robotics, Inc. (Delaware)
**Lead investor:** Harbor Lane Ventures IV, L.P.
**Date:** 14 May 2026

This term sheet is non-binding except for the confidentiality, exclusivity and
expenses provisions.

fully-diluted basis is nearer $43.5M than $52M.

## Economic terms

**Dividends.** 8% non-cumulative, payable when and if declared by the Board.
Non-cumulative means unpaid dividends do not accrue — in practice these are
never declared and the clause matters only as a floor in a negotiated exit.

**Liquidation preference.** 1× non-participating. On a liquidation event, each
holder receives the greater of (a) the original issue price plus declared but
unpaid dividends, or (b) the amount receivable on an as-converted basis.

Non-participating is the market standard and is what the Company should hold
out for. Participating preferred ("double dip") returns the preference *and*
the as-converted share, and materially changes founder outcomes in a modest
exit.

**Conversion.** Convertible at any time at the holder's option. Automatic
conversion on (a) a firm-commitment underwritten public offering with gross
proceeds of at least $50,000,000, or (b) the written consent of holders of a
majority of the Series A.

**Anti-dilution.** Broad-based weighted average. Full-ratchet is off-market at
this stage and would be resisted.

Standard carve-outs from anti-dilution: shares issued under the option pool,
on conversion of the Series A, in connection with equipment financing or
commercial partnerships approved by the Board, and in a public offering.

**Pay-to-play.** A holder that does not participate pro rata in a subsequent
qualified financing has its Series A converted to Common. Both investor and
company should want this — it is what keeps a syndicate honest in a down round.

## Control terms

**Board.** Five seats:
- Two designated by the holders of Common (currently the CEO and CTO)
- One designated by the lead investor
- One designated by the holders of a majority of the Series A
- One independent, mutually agreed

**Protective provisions.** The consent of holders of a majority of the Series A
is required to:

1. Liquidate, dissolve or wind up the Company
2. Effect a merger, sale of substantially all assets, or change of control
3. Amend the Certificate of Incorporation or Bylaws in a manner adverse to the Series A
4. Create or authorise any security senior to or pari passu with the Series A
5. Increase or decrease the authorised number of shares of Preferred or Common
6. Increase the size of the Board
7. Declare or pay any dividend
8. Redeem or repurchase shares, other than at cost from departing service providers
9. Incur indebtedness in excess of $2,000,000 outside an approved budget

**Information rights.** Monthly unaudited financials within 30 days, annual
audited financials within 120 days, an annual budget 30 days before year end,
and a capitalisation table on request. Available to any holder of at least
250,000 shares ("Major Investor").

**Pro-rata rights.** Each Major Investor has the right to participate in
subsequent financings to maintain its as-converted ownership percentage. This
right terminates on an initial public offering.

**Right of first refusal and co-sale.** The Company, then the Major Investors,
have a right of first refusal over transfers of Common by founders and
holders of more than 1% of Common, with a co-sale right on any transfer.

**Drag-along.** Holders of Common, the founders, and the Major Investors agree
to vote for a sale of the Company approved by (a) the Board, (b) holders of a
majority of Common, and (c) holders of a majority of the Series A.

## Founder terms

**Vesting.** Founder shares are subject to a 4-year vest with a 1-year cliff,
with credit given for time served since the founding date (approximately 30
months as of close, so roughly 62.5% vests at close and the remainder over 18
months).

**Acceleration.** Double-trigger on a change of control: 100% acceleration if
the founder is terminated without cause or resigns for good reason within 12
months following the transaction. No single-trigger acceleration.

**Confidential information and invention assignment.** Required of every
current and future employee and consultant, executed prior to closing.

## Conditions to closing

- Satisfactory completion of legal, financial and technical diligence
- Executed Stock Purchase Agreement, Investors' Rights Agreement, Voting
  Agreement, and Right of First Refusal and Co-Sale Agreement
- Delivery of the audited FY2025 financial statements
- Confirmation that all IP developed by founders prior to incorporation has
  been assigned to the Company
- Resolution of the outstanding single-source supplier qualification disclosed
  in the Company's diligence responses
- Key-person insurance on the CEO and CTO, $5,000,000 each, Company as beneficiary

**Expenses.** The Company reimburses the lead investor's legal fees up to
$50,000 on closing.

**Exclusivity.** The Company agrees not to solicit or negotiate an alternative
financing for 30 days from execution.

**Expiration.** This term sheet expires if not executed by 28 May 2026.
