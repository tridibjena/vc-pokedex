## Thesis

Fibrotic disease accounts for roughly 45% of deaths in the industrialised world
and there are four approved antifibrotics, none of which reverse existing
scarring. Cellulon's lead asset, CB-204, is a selective inhibitor that has
demonstrated **reversal**, not merely slowed progression, in two animal models
and one Phase 1b human cohort.

## Phase 1b results (CB-204, n=42)

- Primary endpoint met: dose-dependent reduction in serum PRO-C3 of 34% at
  400mg BID versus 4% placebo (p=0.003)
- Quantitative HRCT showed a mean 2.1% reduction in fibrotic volume at 24 weeks.
  No approved therapy has shown volume reduction in this population.
- Forced vital capacity improved 61mL against a placebo decline of 74mL
- No grade 3+ treatment-related adverse events. Two discontinuations, both for
  grade 2 nausea.
- Half-life 14.2 hours, supporting twice-daily oral dosing

**We would caution:** n=42 is small, the trial was not powered for efficacy, and
imaging was a secondary endpoint at a single site. The effect size is the reason
to do a Phase 2, not evidence of one.

## Market

- IPF: ~140,000 patients in the US, ~3M worldwide
- Current standard of care (nintedanib, pirfenidone) generates $4.1B annually
  despite slowing decline rather than reversing it, and despite discontinuation
  rates above 25% for tolerability
- NASH with F3 fibrosis: ~1.9M US patients, one approved therapy as of 2024
- Analyst consensus peak sales for a disease-modifying antifibrotic: $6–9B

## Competition

**Boehringer Ingelheim, Roche** — own the installed base. Both have
next-generation programs in Phase 2. We assume a fast follower.

**Pliant Therapeutics, Galecto** — closest mechanistic competitors. Pliant's
integrin approach reads out roughly six months ahead of us and is a genuine risk
to our financing environment regardless of our own data.

**Our differentiation.** Selectivity. The class has historically failed on
tolerability, not on target engagement. CB-204's 190-fold selectivity over the
nearest paralog is why our discontinuation rate is 4.8% against 25%+ for
standard of care.

## Risks

- **Binary clinical risk.** Phase 2a is the company. A miss on the interim
  means tranche 2 does not release and we have roughly seven months of cash.
- **Enrolment.** IPF trials compete for a small, sick, heavily-recruited
  population. We are at 61% of target enrolment against a plan of 74%.
- **Competitive readout timing.** Pliant reads out ahead of us. A negative
  result there closes the antifibrotic financing window for everyone,
  irrespective of mechanism.
- **Manufacturing.** Single CDMO for API. Tech transfer to a second site is
  budgeted but not started.
- **Key person.** Dr. Menon is the originator of the chemotype and the
  scientific relationship with two of our academic collaborators.
