## The quarter

Three new investments, two follow-ons, one realisation.

**New investments — $19.4M deployed**

- **Northwind Robotics** (Series A lead, $11.0M at $70M post). Warehouse
  automation retrofitted into existing racking. 141% net revenue retention and a
  1.6× burn multiple at $8.7M ARR. Our thesis is that the leaseholder segment

**Follow-ons — $8.2M**

- **Cellulon Bio** — $6.0M into the $75M Series B, maintaining our pro rata.
  Tranched against the Phase 2a interim. We took the full allocation despite the
  binary risk because the Phase 1b imaging result is the first volume reduction
  anyone has shown in this population.
- **Tessellate** — $2.2M bridge at the prior round's price. Discussed below.

**Realisation**

- **Ferrous Analytics** acquired by Trimble for $84M cash. We invested $4.1M
  across seed and Series A for 21.4% and returned $17.9M, a **4.4× gross**
  multiple over 31 months. Not a fund-returner, but a clean outcome in a
  category that has consolidated faster than we expected.

## Portfolio marks

We mark to the last priced round unless there is a specific impairment or a
clear observable event. We do not mark up on unpriced SAFEs, and we do not mark
up on revenue growth alone. Four positions moved this quarter:

| Company | Prior | Current | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cellulon Bio | 1.0× | 3.1× | Series B priced, third-party led |
| Ferrous Analytics | 2.8× | 4.4× | Realised |
| Halcyon Grid | 2.2× | 2.2× | Held — no new round, growing into the mark |
| Tessellate | 1.8× | 0.4× | Impaired, see below |

**Tessellate.** Our 2023 Series A. Developer tooling for schema migration. ARR
peaked at $4.1M in Q3 2025 and has declined to $2.9M as two large customers
brought the capability in-house. The founding CTO left in March. We wrote the
position down to 0.4× and provided a $2.2M bridge at the prior price to fund a
12-month repositioning toward the managed-service motion that is retaining.

We think this is more likely than not a write-off. We are funding it because
the remaining team is strong, the bridge is small relative to the fund, and the
alternative is a certain zero. We will not commit further capital beyond this
bridge without a priced round led by a third party.

The honest lesson: we underwrote a product in a category where the buyer had a
credible build option and we did not weight that enough. We have added
"could the customer build this, and what stops them?" as an explicit section in
the investment memo template.

which lets us concentrate into the six positions that are working rather than
spreading follow-on capital evenly across a portfolio where it is not warranted.

## Market observations

Series A pricing in our categories has stabilised after two years of
compression. Median pre-money in the deals we saw this quarter was $48M on
$2.4M of ARR, which is a 20× forward multiple — down from 34× in 2021 but up
from the 14× trough in early 2024.

What has genuinely changed is the bar at seed. A seed round now routinely
requires the traction that a Series A required in 2021. We think this is
healthy and it is why our seed entries this year have been at higher revenue
and lower ownership than our 2023 vintage.

We continue to be cautious on application-layer AI companies whose product is a
thin wrapper on a frontier model. We have passed on 31 such companies this year.
We have made three AI investments, all of which own either proprietary data or
a workflow position that survives the next model generation.
